## Precise remaining geometry and limitation

GL15's `UStepD1.excess_new` consumes the actual step but only concludes its deviation after the structural condition `SingleVineVC K ∨ Nonempty (DoubleShape K)` and, in the latter case, a `VineOutsideConn` witness. `UStepD1` permits a `VineCut` and does not itself carry this condition; `UCtx` only supplies Good, AncRel, DartRel and AtomRelU, without collected-object reach/shape connectivity. A reach/collected-object geometric bridge must construct those witnesses for the chosen K to use the deviation in a LOW run. The sharp critical-count loss of 2 further requires proving that critical atoms cannot occur in the removed interior from the source `crit → SGat` and transported collected-object geometry; an arbitrary `crit` has no such property. Existing REG `VHard` contains that fact as a field. These are geometric interface obligations, not additional conclusion-shaped hypotheses for a source theorem. The generic actual-step bound here remains valid without them.

The current `lowIndexPot` uses `Y*L^(R'X)` with fixed `Y`, whereas REG's initial normalization uses `L^(eraseA*Rn + 4η(Rn−1)−ε₀)` and its terminal comparison uses a distinct `(Rn−1)`-dependent exponent. No contradiction has been proved, but no transport of the normalization across variable index and the `R'=0` terminal case has been established either; the low run and terminal comparison cannot be claimed complete. The branch prefactor remains outside the exponent m. The pointwise/real scalar results do not by themselves prove the end-to-end LOW count or the single combined ENNReal inequality.
